Meeting Notes: Madrona IA40 Summit 2026会议笔记：Madrona IA40 峰会 2026

Notes from the Madrona Intelligent Applications 40 (IA40) Summit 2026, a two-day gathering at the Four Seasons Hotel Seattle on September 29–30. The full program is on the IA40 Summit agenda. Below are the sessions that interested me most, in the order they ran.

Agency in the Agentic Era: Building the Next Generation of Apps and Platforms

Keynote. Charles Lamanna, EVP, Copilot, Agents, and Platform, Microsoft, with Matt McIlwain, Managing Director, Madrona.

Harnessing the Value of Harnesses

Panel. Carlos Guestrin, Co-CEO, Noeri; Rahul Patil, CTO, Anthropic. Moderated by Raphaëlle d’Ornano, CEO & Founder, Decoding Discontinuity.

Betting on Agency

VC panel. Jamin Ball, Partner, Altimeter; Sabrina Albert, Partner, Madrona; Dan Grossman, VP, Business & Corporate Development, Amazon. Moderated by Dina Bass, Bloomberg.

When Models Go Rogue: Building AI Systems You Can Actually Trust

Fireside chat. Zico Kolter, Co-founder & Chief Scientist, Gray Swan, with Vivek Ramaswami, Partner, Madrona.

Verifying the Value of AI

Fireside. Carina Hong, Founder & CEO, Axiom, and Raphaëlle d’Ornano, CEO & Founder, Decoding Discontinuity.

The First Frontier: Software’s Agentic Rewrite

Panel. Eno Reyes, Co-founder & CTO, Factory; Taroon Mandhana, CTO, AI & Teamwork, Atlassian; Thomas Dohmke, Co-founder & CEO, Entire. Moderated by Ted Kummert, Venture Partner, Madrona.

The Future of Autonomous Systems

Keynote. Swami Sivasubramanian, VP of Agentic AI & Emerging Technologies, AWS, with Tim Porter, Managing Director, Madrona. Swami’s session on LinkedIn →

The summit wrapped up with closing remarks from Matt McIlwain.

Notable takeaways

  • Harnesses = everything between goals, model capabilities, and end outcomes; capabilities are scaling rapidly, but outcome ambitions are also scaling
  • The fundamental unlock for the agentic enterprise and agentic actions is no longer data, but high-quality context and continuous learning
  • Headless agents, super agents, and horizontal tools will coexist in AI systems; the battle now is for customer engagement and the associated usage data
  • We are moving along a spectrum of delegating authority: security, privacy, and accuracy are top of mind as we scale toward widespread agentic adoption
  • ROI of AI is early for most end user customers, yet ROI feels inevitable based on the frontier adopters

Reflections

What I appreciated most was the breadth of the conversations: from agentic applications and software development to AI safety, verification, autonomous systems, and the infrastructure needed to make these systems genuinely useful and trustworthy. A great couple of days of learning, exchanging ideas, and thinking about what comes next for AI and software. Thank you to the Madrona Ventures team for putting together such a thoughtful gathering.

峰会在 Matt McIlwain 的闭幕致辞中落下帷幕。

值得记下的要点

  • Harness（驾驭层）是目标、模型能力与最终成果之间的一切；模型能力在快速增长，对成果的期待也在同步增长
  • 智能体企业与智能体行动真正的突破口已不再是数据，而是高质量的上下文与持续学习
  • 无头智能体、超级智能体与横向工具将在 AI 系统中共存；眼下的战场是客户互动及随之产生的使用数据
  • 我们正沿着一条授权的光谱前进：在迈向智能体广泛落地的过程中，安全、隐私与准确性始终是首要考虑
  • 对大多数终端用户客户来说，AI 的投资回报还处于早期，但从前沿采用者的表现看，回报似乎是必然的

感想

我最欣赏的是讨论的广度：从智能体应用、软件开发，到 AI 安全、验证、自主系统，再到让这些系统真正好用且可信所需的基础设施。 两天里学到了很多，也交流了想法，思考了 AI 与软件的下一步。感谢 Madrona Ventures 团队精心筹办了这场聚会。
