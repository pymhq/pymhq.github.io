Update (June 2026): once the old research/engineer buckets dissolve, what fills the gap? Boris Cherny sketches a set of function-agnostic archetypes.

A tweet by Boris Cherny (@bcherny), creator of Claude Code, reframes future roles not by job title but by what people actually do:

“As engineering, product, design, DS, etc. melt into a new kind of role, I was reflecting on what roles might look like in the future. For example, when I look at the Claude Code team I see what I think is five archetypes:
1. Prototyper: comes up with brand new ideas; churns out many ideas, most of which don't ship
2. Builder: quickly turns a prototype/idea into production-grade product/infra
3. Sweeper: cleans up the UI, simplifies the code and system, unships, optimizes performance
4. Grower: takes a product that has been built and iterates on it to improve Product-Market Fit
5. Maintainer: owns a mature system to make it secure, reliable, fast, and efficient as it scales
Many people span across 2 roles, and sometimes 3 roles. I also notice that these roles are not really tied to job function… Maybe product roles of the future will look more like this, and less like the domain-specific roles of today?”

This is the natural next step from the MTS idea (see the May 2026 update below): not only do we drop the “researcher vs engineer” label, but we also stop tying roles to job function at all. A healthy team is just a mix of these archetypes, weighted by where the product is in its lifecycle — pre-PMF leans on Prototyper/Builder/Sweeper, while a mature product leans on Sweeper/Grower/Maintainer. It's the builder-first, problem-solver ethos this post argues for, made concrete.

Update (May 2026): the debate found its answer in a job title — "Member of Technical Staff" (MTS).

A viral tweet by Yuchen Jin (@Yuchenj_UW) captures exactly the de-bucketing this post argues for:

“Whoever invented ‘Member of Technical Staff’ was a genius. It filters out Staff/Principal title-maxxers, protects engineering and research from corporate ladder brain, and leaves recruiters staring at LinkedIn like: ‘Is this person L4 or L7?’ MTS is the best title. Happy to be MTS.”

In the replies, OpenAI's Greg Brockman (@gdb) gave the origin story: when starting OpenAI, “we didn't want to bucket people into researchers & engineers. Alan Kay advised that they used ‘Member of Technical Staff’ at Xerox PARC, we loved & adopted it.” That's the same flat, builder-first ethos — one title for everyone solving the problem.

My Take

I have experience in both academia and industry. During school, I was an independent researcher in the lab of an IEEE Fellow. Later, I also played both roles in my professional work. In my view, an engineer is fundamentally a problem solver.

At Amazon, there’s a concept called the “Builder.” We have resources like The Amazon Builders’ Library and events such as Builder’s Day. Regardless of your role, whether you’re an SDE (Software Development Engineer, or as we sometimes say, “Someone Doing Everything”), MLE, Research Engineer, Applied Scientist, Research Scientist, or Product Manager, you are considered a builder delivering products.



Especially in the era of LLMs, the popular idea of "a one-person company" has gained traction. As I mentioned in another blog post, "..a new working model where Product, Engineering, and Research collaborate more closely than ever before". In the new era, everyone can be a product manager, researcher, engineer, or marketer, and the boundaries between professions will become increasingly blurred.

更新（2026 年 6 月）：当旧的"研究员/工程师"分类消解之后，空出来的位置由什么填补？Boris Cherny 勾勒了一组与职能无关的原型。

Claude Code 的创造者 Boris Cherny（@bcherny）的一条推文重新定义了未来的角色——不按职位头衔，而按人们实际做的事：

「随着工程、产品、设计、数据科学等融合成一种新型角色，我一直在思考未来的角色会是什么样。比如，看看 Claude Code 团队，我认为有五种原型：
1. Prototyper（原型者）：提出全新的想法；产出大量点子，其中大多数不会上线
2. Builder（构建者）：快速把原型/想法变成生产级的产品/基础设施
3. Sweeper（清道夫）：清理 UI、简化代码与系统、下线功能、优化性能
4. Grower（增长者）：接手已构建的产品并持续迭代，改进产品与市场的契合度（PMF）
5. Maintainer（维护者）：拥有一个成熟系统，随规模扩展保证其安全、可靠、快速、高效
很多人横跨 2 种角色，有时是 3 种。我还注意到这些角色其实并不绑定于职能……也许未来的产品角色会更像这样，而不是今天这种按领域划分的角色？」

这是 MTS 理念（见下方 2026 年 5 月更新）的自然下一步：我们不仅丢掉了"研究员 vs 工程师"的标签，甚至不再把角色与职能绑定。一个健康的团队，就是这些原型按产品所处生命周期加权的组合——PMF 之前依赖 Prototyper/Builder/Sweeper，成熟产品则依赖 Sweeper/Grower/Maintainer。这正是本文主张的 builder 优先、问题解决者至上的精神，被具体化了。

更新（2026 年 5 月）：这场辩论在一个职位头衔中找到了答案——"Member of Technical Staff"（MTS，技术成员）。

Yuchen Jin（@Yuchenj_UW）的一条爆火推文恰好道出了本文主张的"去分类化"：

「发明『Member of Technical Staff』的人是个天才。它过滤掉了沉迷 Staff/Principal 头衔的人，保护工程和研究免受公司爬梯脑的侵蚀，还让招聘者盯着 LinkedIn 发呆：『这人到底是 L4 还是 L7？』MTS 是最好的头衔。很高兴自己是 MTS。」

在回复中，OpenAI 的 Greg Brockman（@gdb）给出了起源故事：创立 OpenAI 时，「我们不想把人分成研究员和工程师。Alan Kay 建议说，施乐 PARC 用的就是『Member of Technical Staff』，我们很喜欢，就采用了。」这正是同一种扁平的、builder 优先的精神——所有解决问题的人共用一个头衔。

LLM 时代的资历、职级与权威

最近还发生了这样一件事：Meta 任命毕业仅三年的赵晟佳（Shengjia Zhao）为 Meta 超级智能实验室的首席科学家——与 Yann LeCun 的头衔相同，把后者置于一个尴尬的境地，以至于他不得不发文澄清。（同年 11 月，LeCun 离开 Meta，创办了名为 "AMI" 的新创公司。）与此同时，Meta 仍在向 LLM 领域的个人与专家开出巨额薪酬包。LLM 正在迅速而颠覆性地改变世界。这反映出：在 LLM 时代，企业的任期、资历、职级与权威正在被重新定义。


Meta 或许是个极端案例，但也有一些团队保持着冷静——比如 Thinking Machines Lab 的 Andrew Tulloch（该实验室由前 OpenAI 研究副总裁 Lilian Weng 联合创立）。


Anthropic 联合创始人 Ben Mann 也在 Lenny's Podcast 上提到，他并不太担心与 Meta 的这场抢人大战。


我的看法

我在学术界和工业界都有经历。读书时，我在一位 IEEE Fellow 的实验室里做独立研究；后来在职业工作中，两种角色我都扮演过。在我看来，工程师本质上是问题解决者。

Amazon 有个概念叫"Builder（构建者）"。我们有 The Amazon Builders' Library 这样的资源，也有 Builder's Day 这样的活动。无论你的角色是什么——SDE（软件开发工程师，或者按我们有时的说法，"Someone Doing Everything"，什么都干的人）、MLE、研究工程师、应用科学家、研究科学家还是产品经理——你都是交付产品的 builder。



尤其在 LLM 时代，"一人公司"的流行理念获得了广泛认同。正如我在另一篇博客中所说，"……一种产品、工程与研究前所未有紧密协作的新工作模式"。在新时代，每个人都可以是产品经理、研究员、工程师或营销者，职业之间的边界将越来越模糊。
