The site accumulated pictures faster than it accumulated places to keep them. Photographs on this shelf are filed by what they are of, not by which page first used them, and each collection keeps its own captions. Design work and the reels still live on Creativity; the hand-drawn charts are on Maps. 本站积累照片的速度，快过给照片安排位置的速度。这个架子上的照片按拍的是什么归档，而不是按哪一页先用过它，每个合集自带说明文字。设计作品与短片仍在创作页，手绘图仍在地图页。
