  • Salt water 咸水

    Orca. The Southern Residents work the Salish Sea in three pods, J, K and L, and a sighting cannot be scheduled: either you happen to be watching the right stretch of water or you miss it entirely. That is most of the appeal. The Salish Sea chart is drawn around the same water, orcas included. 虎鲸。南部定居型虎鲸分 J、K、L 三个族群，在萨利希海一带活动。遇见这件事没法安排：要么恰好在看对的那片水面，要么就彻底错过。吸引力大半在此。萨利希海航线图画的就是同一片水，虎鲸也在上面。

  • Courts and ice 球场与冰面

    Pickleball was invented in 1965 on Bainbridge Island, one ferry ride from downtown Seattle, which makes playing it here less of a trend than a local custom. Kraken and Seahawks cover the ice and the turf: Climate Pledge Arena at Seattle Center, Lumen Field at the south end of downtown. 匹克球 1965 年诞生于班布里奇岛，从西雅图市中心坐一趟渡轮就到，所以在这里打球更像本地习俗，而不是潮流。Kraken 与 Seahawks 分管冰面与草皮：Climate Pledge Arena 在 Seattle Center，Lumen Field 在市中心南端。

  • Print 纸页

    Kosaku Shima. Kenshi Hirokane has been serialising it since 1983, one salaryman's entire career inside one company, promotion by promotion. It is the only long-running fiction I know that treats an org chart as a plot, which is a strange thing to find comforting after a decade in one place. 岛耕作。弘兼宪史从 1983 年连载至今，讲一个上班族在同一家公司里的整段职业生涯，一级一级升上去。在我读过的长篇里，只有它把组织架构图当情节写，而在同一个地方待满十年之后再读它，竟然是种慰藉。

    Melville is the other end of the shelf. He spent nineteen years as a customs inspector in New York and wrote on the side, an arrangement any engineer who writes will recognise, and Moby-Dick puts the shelf back where this page opened: out on the water, following a whale. It is also the book that ties Pike Place to Nantucket. Starbucks took its name from the coffee-loving first mate, and the island is where the book weighs anchor. 梅尔维尔在书架的另一端。他在纽约当了十九年海关稽查员，写作只是业余，这种安排任何一个写东西的工程师都认得。而《白鲸》又把书架送回本页开头的地方：海上，追着一头鲸。它也是把派克市场和南太基系在一起的那本书：星巴克之名取自书里那位嗜咖啡的大副，而开卷起锚的地方正是那座岛。

  • Screen and headphones 银幕与耳机

    Tarantino for structure. The chapters land out of order and the real reveal is what the order was. Lorde for the opposite move: one voice with a great deal of empty space left around it, and nothing added to fill the space. 昆汀看的是结构。章节乱序落地，真正的揭示是它们原本的顺序。Lorde 是反向操作：一把嗓子，周围留下大量空白，而且不往里填东西。

  • The shelf 架子上

    Magnets and mugs, counted by city rather than by country. It is the cheapest travel log there is and the only one I have never let fall behind. The globe keeps the same habit in another form: every pin carries a date. 冰箱贴和马克杯，按城市数，不按国家数。这是最便宜的旅行记录，也是唯一没被我落下的一份。地球仪用另一种形式延续同一个习惯：每个图钉都带日期。
