An interactive globe of the places I've visited — with hand-drawn Pacific Northwest charts below it. 一个记录我到访之地的交互式地球仪——下方还有手绘的太平洋西北地图。

Drag the globe to spin it: visited countries fill in blue, and every city pin carries a date. Scroll on to the Pacific Northwest panel, then swipe right for the Salish Sea routes, the race-routes chart and a map of all 63 U.S. national parks. Scroll on again for the same Salish Sea chart drawn to scale, on a screen of its own. 拖动地球仪即可旋转：到访过的国家填上蓝色，每个城市图钉都带有日期。继续向下滚动进入太平洋西北面板，再向右滑动查看萨利希海航线图、比赛路线图，以及全美 63 座国家公园地图。继续往下，是同一片萨利希海的等比例版本，单独占满一屏。
