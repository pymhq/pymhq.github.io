Principles设计原则

  • Code is the aesthetic代码即美学Monospace everywhere. The site reads like a well-formatted source file.全站等宽字体。整个网站读起来像一份排版精良的源代码文件。
  • Cards, not pages卡片，而非页面Soft borders, 12px radii, shadows that whisper. Content lives in cards.柔和的边框、12px 圆角、若隐若现的阴影。内容都住在卡片里。
  • Motion earns its place动效物尽其用Reveals, nudges, and bobs — always subtle, always skippable via reduced-motion.渐显、轻推、浮动 —— 始终克制，且始终尊重减少动效偏好设置。
  • Zero frameworks零框架Hand-written HTML/CSS/JS. Every byte on the page was put there on purpose.纯手写 HTML/CSS/JS。页面上的每一个字节都有它存在的理由。

Cartography地图绘制

Style study. Olive land, dashed borders, snow-capped peak glyphs, italic water labels.风格研究。橄榄色陆地、虚线边界、雪顶山峰图标、斜体水域标注。 Open /visitings打开 /visitings

Anatomy of a panel map一幅面板地图的解剖

  • 1A rotatable D3 globe greets you first — countries fill in as they're visited, cities pin with tooltips.首先迎接你的是可旋转的 D3 地球仪 —— 到访过的国家逐一填色，城市图钉带有悬浮提示。
  • 2Scroll down, and the world zooms in — a full-viewport Pacific Northwest chart snaps into place with a fade-and-rise reveal.向下滚动，世界随之放大 —— 一幅满屏的太平洋西北地区海图以渐显上升的方式吸附就位。
  • 3Swipe right for route charts — Salish Sea ferry crossings drawn like an old nautical map: anchors, whales, sea-siren medallions.向右滑动查看航线图 —— 萨利希海的渡轮航线画成旧式航海图的模样：锚、鲸鱼、海妖徽章。
  • 4Everything is inline SVG — no tiles, no map API. Peaks get elevation labels; unvisited ones fade back politely.一切都是内联 SVG —— 没有瓦片，没有地图 API。山峰标注海拔；未到访的则礼貌地淡入背景。
  • 5Dots, chevrons, and edge handles guide the way — the same navigation grammar reused across the whole site.圆点、箭头和边缘把手指引方向 —— 与全站复用同一套导航语法。
